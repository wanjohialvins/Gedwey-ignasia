Overview

Gedwey Ignasia is a private, emotionally intelligent relationship app designed to help couples and people in early dating stages build deeper connection through shared experiences, meaningful conversations, emotional awareness, and real-time interaction.

The app is not just another messaging or dating platform.

It is designed as a:

relationship growth platform
emotional connection tool
shared experience system
daily bonding ritual

Small intentional moments build strong relationships.”

A viral-first feature designed for:

early dating
social sharing
onboarding growth

Shareable link generated
Partner/guest answers

Purpose:

🎴 Session System

The emotional core of the app.

Couples complete shared sessions involving:

questions
moods
reflections
compatibility prompts
emotional check-ins

Both users answer privately before reveal.

This creates anticipation, honesty, and emotional engagement.

A private memory space for couples.

Users can:

Users create messages, photos, or reflections locked until a future date.

Examples:

“Read this in 6 months”
“Future us”

This feature reinforces long-term emotional investment.
